Die hart agter hierdie

Aan die begin van hierdie jaar was sommige van ons vreemdelinge en ander reeds vriende, maar nou is jy deel van die fondasie van my lewe. Ek wil God eer vir hierdie wonderlike vriendskappe wat Hy in ons lewens geplaas het, deur saam te kom vir die breek van brood. Jy is die yster wat my slyp, en ek is so geëerd om jou my vriend te kan noem. Maar dit gaan nie net oor ons vriendskap nie, daarom wil ek graag hê dat jy iemand nooi wat hierdie jaar vir jou beteken het wat jy vir my beteken het, sodat ons almal saam God kan eer en dank vir die verhoudings waarmee Hy ons geseën het.

Logistiek

The heart behind this

At the start of this year, some of us were strangers and others were already friends, but now you are a part of the foundation of my life. I want to honour God for these amazing friendships He has placed in our lives by coming together for the breaking of bread. You are the iron that sharpens me, and I am so honoured to call you my friend. But this is not just about our friendship, so I'd love for you to invite someone who has meant to you this year what you have meant to me, so that we can all honour and thank God together for the relationships He has blessed us with.

Logistics

Could everyone attending please bring food and drinks to share? It doesn't have to be big or fancy, but preferably not just snacks. Please also bring a blanket to sit on.
